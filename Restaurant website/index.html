About Us

Welcome to The Big Bite Restaurant, where culinary excellence meets exceptional hospitality. Nestled in the heart of Kolkata, our restaurant has been a beloved destination for food enthusiasts since 2018. At The Big Bite Restaurant, we are passionate about creating memorable dining experiences. Our talented team of chefs, meticulously crafts each dish using only the finest, locally-sourced ingredients. From classic favorites to innovative creations, our menu offers a tantalizing array of flavors to satisfy every palate. Beyond our culinary offerings, we take pride in providing warm and attentive service to our guests. Whether you're joining us for a romantic dinner, a family celebration, or a casual gathering with friends, our dedicated staff is committed to ensuring your visit is nothing short of exceptional. As a pillar of the local community, we are deeply committed to sustainability and supporting local farmers and artisans. We believe in giving back to the community that has embraced us, and we actively participate in charitable initiatives and events throughout the year.

Thank you for choosing The Big Bite Restaurant. We look forward to welcoming you and creating memorable moments together.
